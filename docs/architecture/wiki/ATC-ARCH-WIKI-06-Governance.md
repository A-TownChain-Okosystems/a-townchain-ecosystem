# Chapter 06 — Governance and change control

**Status:** `PROPOSED / NOT VERIFIED`

## Decision rights

Architecture proposals, implementation state, verification results and normative standards are distinct. A catalog entry may organize and link a requirement but cannot silently promote a proposed rule into a normative requirement.

## Standards authority

Normative changes must follow approved change control in `A-TownChain-Okosystems/atc-standards`. This catalog and Wiki reference standards; they do not replace the normative registry.

## Exceptions and residuals

Every exception needs scope, rationale, risk, accountable owner, approval reference, expiration/review condition where applicable, and affected contracts. Residuals remain visible in reporting and cannot be counted as verified.

## Change discipline

- Preserve established IDs and decision history.
- Record exact commit SHAs for catalog and canonical source.
- No force-push, CI weakening or silent deletion of unresolved records.
- Keep work in Draft while required evidence/review is incomplete.
- Never infer consensus, protocol, security or ownership semantics.
- A layer acceptance decision must enumerate included and excluded IDs and accepted residuals.

## Audit trail

For each material change, record what changed, why, which IDs are affected, source/evidence revisions, validators/tests run, results, reviewer and decision. Reconcile human-readable pages with machine-readable registries before acceptance.

## Current state

This chapter set is a proposed documentation structure. It does not claim that all layers, functions, validators or implementations have been verified.
