### 1.1 MetaMask Agent Wallet — UNCHANGED market state; evidence completeness improved

**Area:** Blockchain, wallets, Web3, AI agents  
**Previous snapshot:** 2026-10-24 — current GA was UNVERIFIED; the June launch page described limited CLI Early Access and a summer 2026 GA target.  
**Current check:** 2026-10-25 — Early Access is confirmed live; GA remains UNVERIFIED.  
**Publication / availability:** MetaMask’s official “now live” page is dated 2026-08-06 and says the Agent Wallet officially launched for accepted Early Access users. This is not a new launch today.

**Verified technical characteristics:** self-custodial wallet; configurable spend limits and protocol allowlists; Guard Mode and opt-in Beast Mode; transaction simulation; Blockaid-powered threat scanning; MEV protection; 2FA approval for flagged or out-of-policy transactions; supported EVM networks and HyperLiquid; gas fees can be settled in the token being moved. MetaMask states that eligible transactions meeting its conditions can receive Transaction Protection coverage up to USD 10,000 per month.

**Commercial data:** no separate Agent Wallet tariff or fee schedule was verified. The coverage statement is a conditional protection benefit, not a price quote.

**Evidence quality:** High for the Early Access launch and described controls; partial for current availability because the same official page still says GA is coming “this summer” without a replacement date. The page is internally stale on the GA timeline, so GA must not be claimed.

**Sources:** [MetaMask Agent Wallet — official page dated 2026-08-06](https://metamask.io/news/introducing-metamask-agent-wallet) · [Earlier MetaMask launch page](https://metamask.io/en-GB/news/metamask-launches-agent-wallet-giving-ai-agents-full-defi-access-with-default-security-on-every-transaction)

**Relevance to A-TownChain — analysis:** High. MetaMask demonstrates that agent-side transaction execution can be constrained at the wallet boundary with simulation, limits, allowlists and step-up approval. The A-TownChain Wallet SSOT should retain transaction simulation, policy limits, allowlists and explicit approval as canonical controls; Aurora must not acquire signing authority by virtue of agent identity or model output.

**Roadmap implication:** Compare native wallet policy enforcement against the MetaMask control set. Keep authorization and signing in the canonical Wallet SSOT and ShivaCore boundary; do not add MetaMask as a runtime dependency.

**Open questions:** Is GA now planned for a new date? Which regions and chains are supported at current Early Access? Are fees or eligibility conditions published separately? Does the current support documentation supersede the stale summer-GA sentence?
