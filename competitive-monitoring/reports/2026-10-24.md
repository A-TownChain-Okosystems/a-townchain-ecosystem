**Roadmap implication:** Review ATC protocol-upgrade governance and evidence schemas; do not copy Ethereum implementation or add a third-party runtime dependency.

**Open questions:** Replacement Hoodi date; client versions whose own release notes confirm the revised configuration; any subsequent mainnet schedule.

**Product area:** Aurora AI, agents, security architecture  
**Publication date:** 2026-10-21
